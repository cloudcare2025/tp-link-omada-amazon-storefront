Omada Cloud Portal

100% cloud-managed networking. Manage every access point, switch, and router across every site from a single browser tab -- no on-premises controller hardware required.

Enterprise Cloud Management

The Omada Cloud Portal provides a unified control plane for your entire network infrastructure. From a single dashboard, configure policies, monitor performance, and deploy new sites without leaving your desk.

Multi-Site Management

Deploy and configure networks across unlimited sites from a single portal. Manage different brands, regions, and business units with centralized visibility and per-site policy control.

Zero-Touch Provisioning

Ship devices directly to remote sites. When powered on and connected to the internet, they automatically enroll in your cloud controller, download their configuration, and join your managed network.

Real-Time Monitoring

Live dashboard showing network status, traffic distribution, client counts, and bandwidth utilization across all sites. Topology maps provide visual troubleshooting for quick issue resolution.

Alerts and Notifications

Abnormal event warnings with configurable thresholds. Receive notifications for device outages, unusual traffic patterns, configuration changes, and security events before they impact users.

MSP Multi-Tenant

Purpose-built for managed service providers. Multi-tenant privilege assignment, customer and site management within MSP mode, and multi-person management with role-based permissions.

API Access

Programmatic access to your network configuration and telemetry data. Integrate with your existing IT management tools, build custom automations, and export data for compliance reporting.

Unified Dashboard Experience

The Omada Cloud Portal dashboard surfaces the metrics that matter. Designed for IT administrators and MSP operators who need actionable intelligence, not data overload.

Network Status Overview

At-a-glance health indicators for every managed device. See online/offline status, CPU and memory utilization, client counts, and uptime across your entire infrastructure. Color-coded alerts flag devices that need attention immediately.

Traffic Distribution Analytics

Understand bandwidth consumption by application, client, SSID, and time period. Identify bottlenecks, optimize channel assignments, and validate QoS policies with historical trend data that informs capacity planning decisions.

Network Topology Mapping

Auto-discovered network topology displays device interconnections, link speeds, and port assignments in a visual map. Click any device node to drill down into configuration details, connected clients, and performance metrics.

Log and Event Management

Centralized logging for network events, configuration changes, and security incidents. Searchable, filterable, and exportable -- giving your compliance and security teams the audit trail they require.

Built for Managed Service Providers

The Omada Cloud Portal is designed from the ground up for MSPs managing hundreds of customer networks. Multi-tenant architecture, role-based access, and operational tools that scale with your client base.

Multi-Tenant Privilege Assignment

Assign granular permissions per customer, per site, and per administrator. Technicians see only the networks they manage. Customers get read-only dashboards without accessing other tenants.

Centralized Remote Management

Manage all customer networks from a single unified visual platform. Push firmware updates, modify configurations, and troubleshoot issues across your entire portfolio without dispatching field technicians.

Scalability and Flexibility

From a single-site coffee shop to a multi-campus enterprise, the platform scales without infrastructure changes. Add sites, devices, and administrators on demand with no capacity planning overhead.

Multi-Level Permissions

Define administrator roles with fine-grained access controls. Super admins oversee the entire portfolio, while site managers control individual locations, and read-only viewers monitor without modification rights.

Omada App Management

Manage your network from anywhere using the Omada mobile app for iOS and Android. Receive push notifications, perform quick configuration changes, and monitor network health on the go.

Batch Configuration

Apply configuration templates across hundreds of devices simultaneously. Standardize SSID naming, VLAN assignments, firewall rules, and QoS policies across your customer base with a single batch operation.
