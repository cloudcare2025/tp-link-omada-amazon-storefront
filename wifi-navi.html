WiFi Navi App

Professional Site Survey & Signal Analysis. Troubleshoot, optimize, and validate your Wi-Fi network with a comprehensive suite of diagnostic tools -- free on iOS and Android.

Diagnostic & Survey Tools

A professional toolkit for pre-installation planning, post-installation validation, and ongoing network troubleshooting.

Signal Strength Mapping

Walk through your site with the Walking Test feature to generate real-time heat maps of Wi-Fi signal strength. Visualize coverage across your entire floor plan and identify weak spots instantly.

Channel Analysis

Scan the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz bands to detect interference from neighboring networks. Identify congested channels and optimize your AP channel assignments for maximum throughput.

Coverage Gap Detection

The integrated Wi-Fi test generates comprehensive coverage reports that pinpoint dead zones and areas with insufficient signal. Get actionable data to close coverage gaps before users report problems.

Performance Testing

Built-in iPerf client integration for in-depth bandwidth testing. Measure real throughput, packet loss, jitter, and latency between any two points on your network with enterprise-grade accuracy.

Report Generation

Generate and export professional diagnostic reports documenting network performance, coverage quality, and identified issues. Share results with clients, stakeholders, or installation teams.

Roaming Test

Assess the stability and efficiency of client device transitions between access points. Monitor packet loss, latency spikes, and handoff time as you move through the coverage area.

Complete Network Diagnostics in Your Pocket

The Omada WiFi Navi App is a free professional mobile application built for network installers, IT administrators, and managed service providers. It consolidates pre-installation planning tools, post-installation validation, and day-to-day troubleshooting into a single app that works with any Wi-Fi network -- not just Omada.

  • Pre-installation: Bandwidth Calculator, PoE Calculator, Interference Detection
  • Post-installation: Walking Test heat maps, Roaming Test validation
  • Troubleshooting: Wi-Fi Integrated Test, Ping Test, Trace Route
  • Performance: iPerf client for throughput, jitter, and packet loss testing
  • Network Utilities: IP Scanner, Port Scanner, Public IP Lookup
  • Daily Use: Internet Speed Test, Device Scanning, Wi-Fi Scanning
  • Works with any Wi-Fi network -- not limited to Omada
  • Available free on iOS and Android
