Omada Design Hub

Professional Network Planning Tool. Design, simulate, and optimize your Wi-Fi deployment with AI-powered floor plan analysis and heat map generation -- completely free.

Powerful Planning Features

Everything you need to design a professional-grade network deployment, from initial floor plan import through final bill of materials.

Floor Plan Import

Upload architectural floor plans in common formats. The AI engine automatically detects and draws walls, rooms, and structural elements to build your site model.

Heat Map Generation

Visualize Wi-Fi signal coverage across your entire floor plan with color-coded heat maps. Identify dead zones and optimize AP placement before a single device is installed.

Coverage Optimization

AI-powered algorithms suggest optimal access point placement based on wall materials, room dimensions, and expected client density. Eliminate coverage gaps automatically.

Equipment Selection

Choose from the full Omada product portfolio -- access points, switches, gateways, and controllers. Each device is modeled with real performance characteristics and coverage patterns.

Bill of Materials

Automatically generate a comprehensive bill of materials with device counts, model numbers, and estimated equipment costs. Export directly for procurement and project planning.

Export & Share

Generate professional delivery reports with topology maps, device lists, and coverage visualizations. Share designs with stakeholders or export for installation teams.

Design Your Network in 3 Steps

From floor plan to deployment-ready design in minutes -- no specialized training required.

1
Upload Floor Plan

Import your architectural floor plan. The AI engine automatically detects walls and room boundaries, then sets the scale for accurate coverage simulation.

2
Configure Access Points

Select Omada devices from the product portfolio and place them on the floor plan. Customize wall types, cable routing preferences, and client density requirements.

3
Generate Report

Simulate coverage with heat maps, review the bill of materials, and export a professional deployment report complete with topology diagrams and equipment specifications.

AI-Powered Network Design

The Omada Design Hub is a free, browser-based network planning tool that eliminates guesswork from Wi-Fi deployments. Built on TP-Link's AI engine, it automates the most time-consuming aspects of network design -- wall detection, AP placement optimization, and coverage prediction -- so you can move from concept to installation in a fraction of the time.

  • Free to use -- no subscription, no license fees
  • AI-driven wall detection from uploaded floor plans
  • Real-time Wi-Fi heat map simulation
  • Full Omada product portfolio integrated
  • Customizable wall types and material attenuation
  • Professional PDF report generation
  • Topology map with cable routing visualization
  • Equipment cost estimation and BOM export
