Hotels & Hospitality

The Challenge: Modern hotels must provide flawless Wi-Fi to meet guest expectations, which directly impacts reviews and revenue. This requires full-property coverage, high performance for streaming and work, and secure, easy-to-manage networks for both guests and hotel operations.

The Omada Solution: An end-to-end hospitality solution combining high-performance in-room wall-plate APs, high-density ceiling APs for common areas, and outdoor APs for pools and patios. The Omada SDN platform centralizes management, enabling branded captive portals for guest login, PMS integration for authenticated access, and VLAN segmentation to securely separate guest traffic from hotel administrative systems.

  • Full Wi-Fi coverage with tailored APs (in-room, ceiling, outdoor)
  • High-performance Wi-Fi 6 for superior guest experience
  • Customizable captive portal with voucher, SMS, and social login
  • Seamless roaming for uninterrupted connectivity property-wide
  • VLAN segmentation for guest, staff, IoT, and IPTV traffic
  • Zero-Touch Provisioning for rapid deployment
  • Centralized cloud management for multi-property administration

Recommended: ER7206 Gateway, SG3428MP PoE+ Switch, EAP655-Wall (In-Room), EAP670 (Lobby/Conference), OC300 Controller

Multi-Dwelling Units

The Challenge: Apartments, dorms, and senior living facilities need reliable, scalable, and secure Wi-Fi in high-density multi-tenant environments. Network management is complex and residents expect a private, seamless online experience equivalent to their own home broadband.

The Omada Solution: A comprehensive, integrated networking portfolio managed under the unified Omada SDN platform. Centralized cloud-based management simplifies security with private connections for each unit. Flexible onboarding lets residents and guests self-provision through branded captive portals, while wall-plate APs deliver dedicated coverage per unit without the interference problems of corridor-based deployments.

  • Comprehensive hardware portfolio (APs, switches, gateways, controllers)
  • Omada SDN platform for unified management
  • Simplified security with per-unit private connections
  • Flexible resident onboarding and self-service portals
  • Easy deployment with Zero-Touch Provisioning
  • Future-ready Wi-Fi 6 and Wi-Fi 7 technology
  • Seamless coverage with wall-plate and outdoor APs
  • Efficient operations and maintenance at scale

Recommended: EAP775-Wall / EAP655-Wall (In-Unit), EAP772-Outdoor (Common Areas), 2.5G/10G Uplink Switches, Cloud-Based Controller

Small & Mid-Size Offices

The Challenge: Small to medium-sized offices require a simple, secure, and stable networking solution that can be managed without dedicated IT staff, while reliably supporting business-critical applications like VoIP, video conferencing, and IP security cameras.

The Omada Solution: A unified network management platform that integrates routers, switches, and access points under centralized management (cloud, hardware, or software). Reliable Wi-Fi, seamless VoIP and CCTV integration, and simplified deployment with Zero-Touch Provisioning make it possible to build and maintain an enterprise-grade network without a full-time networking team.

  • Centralized management via cloud, hardware, or software controller
  • Reliable and stable connectivity for business-critical apps
  • Secure Wi-Fi with WPA3 and VLAN segmentation
  • Scalability from 5 to 500+ users
  • Seamless roaming across floors and conference rooms
  • VPN support for secure remote access
  • VoIP and CCTV integration with QoS prioritization
  • Simplified setup and ongoing maintenance
  • Bandwidth management and rate limiting per user group

Recommended: OC200/OC300 Controller, ER605 Gateway, SG2210P Switch, EAP670 Access Point

Chain Restaurants & Stores

The Challenge: Retail businesses with multiple locations require a networking solution that can be managed centrally, provides secure and uninterrupted connectivity for Point-of-Sale systems, and delivers a seamless Wi-Fi experience for both customers and staff across every location.

The Omada Solution: A cloud-based SDN platform that enables centralized management of all store locations from a single interface. Seamless Wi-Fi, enhanced security for payment data transmission, and streamlined operations simplify the opening of new stores. 4G gateway failover ensures POS systems stay online even during ISP outages.

  • Centralized cloud management across all store locations
  • Seamless Wi-Fi with Wi-Fi 7/6 APs and mesh technology
  • Enhanced security with VPN, VLANs, and PCI DSS compliance
  • Zero-Touch Provisioning for rapid new store deployment
  • Uninterrupted POS with 4G failover and multi-WAN load balancing
  • Custom captive portal for customer authentication and branding
  • MSP mode for multi-store management by service providers
  • AI-driven network analysis, alerts, and optimization

Recommended: Cloud-Based Controller, Wi-Fi 6/7 Ceiling-Mount APs, PoE Switches, VPN Gateways with 4G Failover

Warehouses

The Challenge: Warehouses present unique Wi-Fi challenges: vast open spaces, numerous physical obstructions like metal shelving, high ceilings, and the need for seamless, uninterrupted roaming for mobile devices such as handheld barcode scanners and Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs).

The Omada Solution: A robust SDN solution featuring directional access points for targeted, long-range coverage. Advanced features reduce signal interference in metal-rich environments. Seamless roaming ensures handheld devices and AGVs maintain connectivity as they move through the facility. Durable hardware is designed for harsh industrial environments, and centralized management simplifies deployment and maintenance across large facilities.

  • Full Wi-Fi coverage with directional and high-power APs
  • Reduced interference with band steering and BSS coloring
  • Seamless roaming for handheld scanners and AGVs
  • High-density support for hundreds of concurrent devices
  • High security networking with WPA3 and ACLs
  • Open API for WMS and inventory system integration
  • Target Wake Time (TWT) for IoT sensor battery optimization
  • Cost-effective scaling with centralized management

Recommended: OC200/OC300 Controller, Directional APs, Industrial Switches, Outdoor APs, Gigabit VPN Gateways

Farms, Ranches & Parking Facilities

The Challenge: Agricultural and large outdoor properties require network connectivity over vast, open areas for modern applications like remote equipment monitoring, security surveillance, and data collection. Traditional cabling is impractical and prohibitively expensive across these distances.

The Omada Solution: The TP-Link Pharos series provides long-range wireless solutions using Point-to-Point (PtP) and Point-to-Multipoint (PtMP) bridges to transmit data over distances of several kilometers. These weatherproof devices create a wireless backbone connecting remote barns, sheds, fields, and parking structures back to the main network, enabling IP cameras and IoT devices across the entire property.

  • Long-range network extension to remote locations (km-scale)
  • Point-to-Point and Point-to-Multipoint wireless bridges
  • Line-of-sight links with professional-grade antennas
  • Power over Ethernet for flexible outdoor installation
  • IP65/IP67 weatherproof enclosures
  • Scalable architecture for expanding coverage
  • Centralized configuration and firmware management
  • Zero-Touch Provisioning and remote troubleshooting

Recommended: CPE510/CPE610 Wireless Bridges, EAP650-Outdoor APs, Managed PoE Switches, OC200/OC300 Controller

Outdoor WiFi

The Challenge: Extend reliable wireless coverage to outdoor areas such as patios, gardens, swimming pools, event spaces, and building perimeters while protecting equipment from rain, dust, extreme temperatures, and UV exposure.

The Omada Solution: Durable, weatherproof (IP65/IP67) access points designed to withstand harsh conditions. Omada Mesh technology enables flexible deployment without running cables to every AP location. High-gain antennas and Wi-Fi 6 ensure long-range, high-performance coverage. Centralized cloud management through the Omada SDN platform makes maintenance as simple as managing indoor APs.

  • Durable weatherproof enclosures (IP65, IP66, IP67 rated)
  • Long-range coverage with high-power amplifiers and professional antennas
  • Omada Mesh technology for cable-free network extension
  • Seamless roaming between indoor and outdoor zones
  • Centralized cloud management via Omada SDN
  • PoE powered for flexible installation without local power

Recommended: EAP650-Outdoor (AX3000), EAP225-Outdoor (AC1200), EAP110-Outdoor (300Mbps)

High-Density WiFi

The Challenge: Transportation hubs, conference centers, stadiums, and large venues face extreme network congestion from thousands of simultaneous users and devices. Standard Wi-Fi deployments collapse under this load, delivering slow speeds and a poor user experience.

The Omada Solution: Wi-Fi 6 access points leveraging OFDMA, MU-MIMO, and BSS Coloring to dramatically increase network capacity and reduce interference. High-end APs with multi-gigabit Ethernet uplinks, combined with intelligent Load Balancing and Airtime Fairness, ensure a smooth and stable connection for every user -- even with thousands of concurrent clients. The entire network is managed centrally via the Omada SDN platform.

  • Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) with OFDMA and MU-MIMO
  • BSS Coloring for reduced co-channel interference
  • Airtime Fairness to prevent slow clients from degrading performance
  • Intelligent load balancing across APs
  • Band steering to optimize 5 GHz utilization
  • Omada Mesh technology for flexible deployment
  • Seamless roaming across large venues
  • Centralized cloud management via Omada SDN
  • Secure guest networks with captive portal
  • Multi-gigabit Ethernet uplinks (PoE+)

Recommended: EAP690E HD (AXE11000 Tri-Band Wi-Fi 6E), EAP670 (AX5400), EAP660 HD (AX3600)
