VPN & Load Balancing Capabilities

Every Omada gateway supports a comprehensive suite of VPN protocols and intelligent traffic management features to keep your business connected and secure.

VPN Protocols

IPsec VPN (Site-to-Site and Client-to-Site), L2TP/PPTP Server and Client, SSL VPN, OpenVPN, WireGuard VPN, One-Click Auto IPsec VPN, and VPN Passthrough. Secure remote access and multi-site connectivity with enterprise-grade encryption.

Load Balancing & Failover

Multi-WAN Load Balancing, Link Backup (Failover), Policy-based Routing, Session-based Balancing, and Application-based Routing. Ensure maximum uptime with intelligent traffic distribution across multiple WAN connections.

Advanced Security

SPI Firewall, DoS Defense, Deep Packet Inspection (DPI), Intrusion Prevention and Detection System (IPS/IDS), and Access Control Lists (ACL). Protect your network from threats with multiple layers of defense.

Omada SDN Integration

Every gateway integrates seamlessly into the Omada SDN platform for centralized cloud management, zero-touch provisioning, and unified control of your entire network from a single pane of glass.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Omada gateways, VPN configuration, and load balancing.

You can add an Omada Gateway via the Omada SDN Controller. The process involves connecting the gateway to your network, discovering it in the controller interface, and then adopting it to apply centralized configuration. The gateway will automatically receive its settings from the cloud.

Check for IP address conflicts between the gateway and other devices on your network. Ensure the controller can reach the gateway and that the gateway's firmware is compatible with your controller version. You may need to manually set the controller's inform URL on the gateway.

Load Balancing can be configured in the Omada Controller under the Transmission > Load Balancing section. You can enable it and set policies for how traffic is distributed across multiple WAN ports, including session-based, policy-based, and application-based routing rules.

Omada routers support a wide range of VPN protocols, including IPsec, L2TP, PPTP, SSL, OpenVPN, and WireGuard. This enables secure connections for remote employees, site-to-site networks, and branch office connectivity with enterprise-grade encryption and authentication.

For small offices, the ER605 provides reliable multi-WAN routing at an entry-level price. Mid-size businesses benefit from the ER7206 or ER707-M2 with 2.5G uplinks. Enterprise deployments requiring 10G throughput should consider the ER8411. For all-in-one simplicity, the ER7212PC combines a router, PoE+ switch, and controller in one device.
