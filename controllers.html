Omada Controllers

Three ways to manage your network. Cloud-based, hardware, or software -- choose the controller deployment that fits your infrastructure, budget, and scale requirements.

Choose Your Management Platform

Every Omada deployment starts with a controller. Whether you prefer zero-hardware cloud management, a dedicated on-premises appliance, or free software on your own server, Omada gives you full centralized control.

Cloud-Based Controller

100% cloud-managed with unlimited scale, 99.99% SLA, and Zero-Touch Provisioning. No hardware required. Per-device licensing starting at $9.99/device/year.

Hardware Controller

Dedicated on-premises appliances for environments requiring local control. OC200 manages up to 100 APs; OC300 scales to 500 APs with 15,000 clients.

Software Controller

Free software installed on your Windows or Linux server. Manages up to 10,000 devices with full feature parity. Ideal for IT teams that want complete on-premises control at zero cost.

Cloud-Based Controller

The Omada Cloud-Based Controller exists entirely in the cloud, providing centralized management of access points, JetStream switches, and routers from a single interface -- with no on-premises hardware investment.

Unlimited Scale

No ceiling on the number of managed devices. Add access points, switches, and routers across unlimited sites without capacity constraints.

99.99% SLA

Enterprise-grade availability backed by 24/7 automated fault detection and geographically isolated backup servers. Your management plane stays online.

Zero-Touch Provisioning

Deploy remote sites without sending an engineer. Devices auto-configure when connected to the internet, reducing deployment costs and time to operation.

Real-Time Monitoring

Dashboard with live network status, traffic distribution analytics, topology mapping, and abnormal event warnings for proactive issue resolution.

Privacy-First Architecture

Network management data is separated from user traffic. No user data passes through the cloud -- only configuration and telemetry travel over the management channel.

Multi-Site Management

Manage all locations from one portal. MSP-ready with multi-tenant privilege assignment and batch configuration across distributed networks.

Hardware Controllers

Dedicated on-premises appliances for organizations that require local control, air-gapped environments, or compliance mandates that restrict cloud-based management.

OC200 Hardware Controller

Compact controller for small-to-mid-size deployments. Manages up to 100 Omada access points, 20 JetStream switches, and 10 Omada routers.

  • ✓ 2 x 10/100 Mbps Ethernet ports
  • ✓ 1 x USB 2.0 port for auto backup
  • ✓ 802.3af/at PoE or Micro USB powered
  • ✓ Compact: 3.9 x 3.9 x 1.0 inches
  • ✓ Free cloud access & Omada App

OC300 Hardware Controller

Enterprise-grade controller for large deployments. Manages up to 500 Omada access points, 100 JetStream switches, and 100 Omada routers with support for 15,000 concurrent clients.

  • ✓ 2 x Gigabit Ethernet ports
  • ✓ 1 x USB 3.0 port for faster backups
  • ✓ 100-240V AC powered, rack-mountable
  • ✓ Up to 15,000 concurrent clients
  • ✓ Metal casing, durable construction

Frequently Asked Questions

The Omada Cloud-Based Controller (CBC) is a controller that exists entirely in the cloud for the Omada Software Defined Networking (SDN) platform. It provides centralized cloud management of gateways, switches, and access points from a single interface, eliminating the need for on-premises hardware controllers.

The Omada CBC offers centralized cloud management for Omada access points, JetStream switches, and Omada routers, all controlled from a single interface. There is no additional hardware controller investment or installation required on customer premises, and it supports Zero-Touch Provisioning for remote deployments.

Each device managed on the Omada Cloud-Based Controller requires an activated license. You can visit the Omada Cloud website, log into your TP-Link ID, and purchase licenses for your Omada devices. Pricing ranges from approximately $9.99 to $13.44 per device per year depending on the plan.

Yes, each TP-Link ID is eligible for a one-time free trial, with 10 licenses valid for 30 days. You can apply for this on the license subscription website to evaluate the cloud management platform before committing.

You can unbind a license from one device and assign it to another. However, the license timer continues after activation regardless of whether it is bound to a device, and unbinding is limited to a set number of times. Plan your device assignments accordingly.

Not all Omada products currently support the Cloud-Based Controller. Check the official "Devices Supported by Omada Cloud-Based Controller" compatibility list on the TP-Link website to confirm your specific hardware models are supported before purchasing licenses.
